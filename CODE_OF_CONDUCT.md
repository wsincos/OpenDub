# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or contribution type.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Discuss technical disagreement with evidence.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Give attribution to upstream work and contributors.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information, voice data, media, credentials, or confidential material without permission.
- Submitting copyrighted or unauthorized media as project assets.
- Misrepresenting model results, benchmarks, licenses, or authorship.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed by project maintainers.
